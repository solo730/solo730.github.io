CLF-C02 Practice Questions by Domain

Practice questions for the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02) exam, organized by the four exam domains. Every question explains why the right answer is right and why each wrong option is wrong.

The exam has 65 questions and a 90-minute time limit. Scores are scaled from 100 to 1,000, and 700 is a passing score.

The four exam domains

57 sample questions across the four domains. Time your practice by exam weighting: Cloud Technology and Services and Security and Compliance together are about two-thirds of the scored content.

  • Domain 1: Cloud Concepts

    About 24% of the exam, 15 questions

    Benefits of the cloud, the Well-Architected Framework, migration strategies, and cloud economics.

  • Domain 2: Security and Compliance

    About 30% of the exam, 15 questions

    The shared responsibility model, IAM, security services such as GuardDuty and Inspector, and compliance resources.

  • Domain 3: Cloud Technology and Services

    About 34% of the exam, 15 questions

    Global infrastructure, compute, storage, databases, networking, and AI services. The largest domain.

  • Domain 4: Billing, Pricing, and Support

    About 12% of the exam, 12 questions

    EC2 pricing models, cost management tools, AWS Organizations billing, and AWS Support.

How to study for the CLF-C02

Start with the biggest domains

Cloud Technology and Services (34%) and Security and Compliance (30%) carry the most weight. Learn what each core service does and what it is not, since many wrong options are real services that solve a different problem.

Read the explanations for wrong answers

The exam is mostly about telling similar services and concepts apart. Each explanation here covers every option, so a missed question shows you the difference.

Confirm against the official exam guide

AWS updates its services and exam guide over time. Check the current AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner page for the latest domains and weights.
